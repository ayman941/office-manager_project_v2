The Business Challenge

Legacy office administration creates operational bottlenecks, leading to administrative overhead and high operational friction.

running_with_errors

Attendance Discrepancies

Without synchronized check-ins, employees suffer from misrecorded shift calculations. Resolving attendance disputes demands hours of manual auditing from HR professionals.

food_bank

Canteen Food Waste

Canteens operate without accurate daily meal reservations. Chefs prepare excess food due to lack of visibility, resulting in substantial supply chain waste and financial loss.

lock_reset

Security & Compliance Gaps

Manual spreadsheets lack transparent access control. Critical modifications, such as changing attendance hours or leave approvals, lack a persistent, tamper-evident audit log.

Functional & Operational Requirements

Core system specifications established during the requirements elicitation phase to ensure operational efficiency.

check_circle Double-Tier Leave Approvals

Managers retain primary approval authority over team schedules. HR acts as the secondary validation tier, verifying remaining holiday allocation balances before finalizing items for monthly payroll schedules.

history Immutable HR Override Trails

If an employee forgets to clock in, HR Managers can force log insertions. To comply with transparency regulations, every override requires mandatory text reasons, logged permanently.

restaurant Real-Time Food Queue Tracking

Meal preparation orders must update live to synchronize expectations. Employees can track lunch statuses while canteen chefs see exact prep counts to avoid stock overruns.

inventory_2 Supply Threshold Alerts

Canteen ingredients require stock safeguards. The dashboard must issue visual alerts immediately when stock of any buffet item declines to 25% or below.

Design Architecture Trade-offs

Balanced decisions that shaped the production model of the portal.

timeline

Waterfall vs. Agile

Choosing Waterfall guaranteed exact requirements gathering, deterministic leave calculation rules, and strict audit log specifications before any codebase edits. The drawback was deferred user testing and validation loops.

database

Store-Driven API Architecture

All views sync via state stores rather than local component states. For example, the status of a food order is maintained in `useOrderStore` and mapped immediately to employee and canteen lists. This introduces boilerplate, but prevents divergent state bugs.

credit_card_off

Internal Tracking vs. Billing

The order catalog and consumption dashboards operate purely for audit inventory management and cost calculations. Excluding direct billing Gateways reduces payroll complexity and legal constraints, focusing strictly on asset allocations.
